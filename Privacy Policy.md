# M.A.R.I. — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M.A.R.I. (Marwan's Assistant for Reminders & Intelligence), a Discord application, collects, uses, stores, and processes information when you interact with it.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the configuration of the Discord server, M.A.R.I. may process the following information:

### Discord Account and Server Information

* Discord user ID.
* Discord server ID.
* Discord channel ID, where necessary for functionality.
* Display name or username, where needed to provide the service.
* User roles or gaming-related preferences, where collected for supported features.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to operate the features of the application.

### Messages and User-Provided Content

M.A.R.I. may process messages or other content that users explicitly submit to the application or that is necessary for an enabled feature.

This may include messages sent through commands, conversations with the bot, or information provided for gaming reminders and personalization.

M.A.R.I. does not require users to submit passwords, Discord authentication credentials, or other sensitive account credentials.

### Gaming and Personalization Information

If enabled, M.A.R.I. may store information such as:

* Valorant role and agent preferences.
* Match attendance and reminder-related information.
* User-approved jokes or personalization preferences.
* User-provided reasons or context related to match attendance.
* Other information explicitly provided for supported personalization features.

The specific data stored depends on the features enabled and the user's interactions with the application.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

* Provide reminders and attendance functionality.
* Respond to user commands and conversations.
* Generate AI-powered responses.
* Support user-approved personalization features.
* Maintain and improve the reliability and security of the application.
* Respond to support requests and data deletion requests.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use Discord data for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.
